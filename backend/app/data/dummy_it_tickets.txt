Ticket ID: IT-1002
Category: VPN connection issues
Issue: The connection drops every five minutes when I work from home.
Resolution: This is typically caused by a MTU (Maximum Transmission Unit) mismatch on your home router. Open the VPN client network settings and change the MTU size from 1500 to 1400. Reconnect the VPN and monitor for stability.

Ticket ID: IT-1003
Category: VPN connection issues
Issue: I get a timeout error 809 when trying to connect to the main gateway.
Resolution: Error 809 indicates that the VPN port is being blocked by your local firewall. Disable any third-party antivirus firewalls temporarily to test the connection. If successful, add an exception for UDP port 500 in your security software.

Ticket ID: IT-1004

Ticket ID: IT-1005
